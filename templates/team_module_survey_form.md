# Team Member Survey Form (Copy & Paste to your Team)

> **Instructions for Documentation Maintainer:**  
> Copy the message template below and send it to each software/firmware developer on your team (via WhatsApp, Slack, Discord, or Email). When they reply, copy their answers directly into `docs/01-setup-and-installation.md` and `docs/02-system-architecture.md`!

---

### 📩 Message to send to teammates:

```text
Hey team! 👋
I am maintaining our official software documentation and user guide for the project. Could you please reply with answers to these quick questions for your module(s)? It will only take 2 minutes:

1. What module(s) are you developing? (e.g. GUI, Serial Comm, Sensor Acquisition, Motor Control)
2. In 2-3 sentences, what does your module do and what is its main responsibility?
3. What programming language and exact version are you using? (e.g. Python 3.11, C++17)
4. What key libraries/frameworks and versions are you using? (e.g. PyQt6 6.6.1, pyserial 3.5, PlatformIO)
5. How does your module connect or talk to other parts of the system? (e.g., UART COM port, Qt Signal, function call)
6. What is the exact command you run to build or run your code? (e.g., `python main.py` or `pio run --target upload`)
7. Are there any known limitations, edge cases, or bugs we should document?

Thanks a lot for your help! 🚀
```
